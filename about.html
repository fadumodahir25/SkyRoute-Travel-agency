Our Story

Born from a Passion for Adventure

SkyRoute was founded in 2010 by Daniel Mercer, a passionate traveller who believed that everyone deserves to experience the world — without the stress of planning.

What started as a small office in London has grown into one of the UK's most trusted travel agencies, with a team of expert travel consultants who have personally visited every destination we offer.

We don't just book trips — we craft experiences tailored to you.
